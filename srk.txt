Shah Rukh Khan, often referred to as the "Badshah of Bollywood", is one of the most prolific actors in Indian cinema.
He has appeared in more than 80 Hindi films and earned numerous accolades, including 14 Filmfare Awards.
He is frequently cited in the media as one of the most successful film stars in the world.
In 2005, the Government of India honored him with the Padma Shri, and the Government of France awarded him the Ordre des Arts et des Lettres.
He began his career with appearances in several television series in the late 1980s.
He made his Bollywood film debut in 1992 with Deewana.
Early in his career, Khan was recognized for portraying villainous roles in films such as Darr and Baazigar.
He subsequently rose to prominence by playing romantic roles in a series of films, including Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge and Kuch Kuch Hota Hai.
He is also the co-chairman of the motion picture production company Red Chillies Entertainment.
Off-screen, he is known for his philanthropic endeavors and humanitarian work, earning him the UNESCO Champions for Pyramidal Growth award.
